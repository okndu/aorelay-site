Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026 · AO Relay LLC · https://aorelay.com

This Privacy Policy describes how AO Relay LLC (“AO Relay,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information in connection with this website and our work-related communications with employees and job applicants. AO Relay is an independent last-mile package delivery company. This website is not operated by any e-commerce marketplace or delivery network.

1. Who we are

AO Relay LLC is a last-mile package delivery partner serving Hyattsville, Maryland and the greater Washington, DC area. On company invoices the business also appears as A&O Relay LLC.

Business address: 5801 Columbia Park Road, Hyattsville, MD 20785
Email: info@aorelay.com
Phone: (301) 385-0694

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information, such as your name, email address, postal address, and mobile phone number.
  • Employment and application information you provide when you apply for a role or join our team.
  • SMS opt-in consent, including the wording you agreed to, the date, and the mobile number you provided.
  • Limited technical information if you visit this website, such as browser type or pages viewed, if our hosting provider logs that data for security and operations.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate our business, communicate with employees and applicants, consider applications, meet legal and safety obligations, and improve our operations. We do not use this website to send marketing texts to the public.

4. SMS messaging

AO Relay Work Notifications

If you are an employee or applicant and you opt in, AO Relay may send you work-related text messages at the mobile number you provide. These messages may include route-ready notices, report-time and schedule changes, scorecard posting notices, safety reminders, and other employment-related operational updates. This is not a marketing program.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out. After you opt out, we will send a confirmation and then stop work-related texts to that number, except as required by law or to confirm a later request to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help. You can also email info@aorelay.com or call (301) 385-0694.
  • Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

We may use a text-messaging service provider (such as Twilio) to deliver messages on our behalf. Those providers may process phone numbers and message content only to send, deliver, and support the messages we request, and to honor opt-out and help requests.

Consent to receive texts is not a condition of applying for a job or of employment, unless a particular message is required for safety or operational reasons that we communicate separately. You may opt in by signing or checking the consent language on our onboarding or application paperwork. We keep a record of that consent.

5. How we share information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate (for example, hosting, payroll, or messaging vendors), with professional advisors, or when required by law, safety, or a valid legal process. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share or sell mobile numbers or SMS opt-in consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

6. Retention

We keep information for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including employment, legal, safety, and recordkeeping requirements. SMS opt-in and opt-out records are retained as needed to honor your preferences and to meet compliance obligations.

7. Your choices

You may email us to update your contact information or ask questions about this policy. For text messages, reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help at any time. Website browsers may also offer controls for cookies or similar technologies if our host uses them.

8. Children

This website and our work-related messaging program are intended for adults, including job applicants and employees. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. The current version will be posted at https://aorelay.com/privacy.html.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or our SMS program may be sent to info@aorelay.com, or by mail to AO Relay LLC, 5801 Columbia Park Road, Hyattsville, MD 20785, or by phone at (301) 385-0694.
